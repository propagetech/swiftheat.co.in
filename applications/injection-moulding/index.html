Products for injection moulding

A shortlist, not the whole catalogue.

  • Ceramic and Mica Band Heaters Barrels, cylinders and pipes. One piece, split, expandable or partial coverage. Part code BH
  • Ceramic and Mica Nozzle Heaters Injection nozzles and short cylindrical sections with no room for a full band. Part code NZ
  • Coil Heaters Hot runner nozzles and manifolds. Wound to the diameter, profiled along the length. Part code CO

Application notes

Thermocouple placement in a manifold

A manifold thermocouple that sits in the steel rather than close to the flow channel reports a temperature the melt never sees. When the controller then holds that number, the material either degrades or freezes off at the gate. Tell us the distance from the seating face to where the junction has to sit, and which end the cable has to leave from.

Band clamping on a worn barrel

Barrels wear oval and they wear undersize. A band sized to the machine's original drawing grips at two points and heats air at the rest. Measure the barrel where the heater sits, and if the machine is old, say so: a spring loaded or wedge lock clamping style will hold pressure where a plain strap will not.

Bore fit in mould and platen work

Cartridge heater life in a mould is decided almost entirely by the clearance between the heater and the hole. Send the bore diameter and its tolerance from your own drawing. We will size the heater to that fit rather than shipping a standard part and hoping it is close enough.

What to send us

An enquiry with these in it can be quoted the same day. An enquiry without them takes three phone calls first.

  • Machine make, model and clamping force
  • Barrel diameter measured at each zone, and how worn it is
  • Zone temperatures the process actually runs at
  • Nozzle outside diameter and the bore it passes through
  • Number of hot runner zones and the manifold drawing if you have it
  • Bore diameter and tolerance for any mould or platen cartridges
  • What the controller is set to read, Type J or Type K

A photograph of the old element next to a tape measure is worth more than a paragraph of description, and a drawing is worth more than both.
