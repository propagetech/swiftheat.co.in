What we make

Eight families. Every one of them is made to a size, a wattage and a set of options you choose, and every option carries a code that follows the part from enquiry to delivery.

  • Cartridge Heaters Bore mounted in moulds, platens and dies. Round, swaged, made to your length. Part code CH
  • Coil Heaters Hot runner nozzles and manifolds. Wound to the diameter, profiled along the length. Part code CO
  • Ceramic and Mica Band Heaters Barrels, cylinders and pipes. One piece, split, expandable or partial coverage. Part code BH
  • Ceramic and Mica Nozzle Heaters Injection nozzles and short cylindrical sections with no room for a full band. Part code NZ
  • Strip Heaters Flat and gently curved surfaces, sealing bars, platens and duct air. Part code SH
  • Tubular Heaters Air, liquids and surfaces. Straight, U form, W form or coiled to your drawing. Part code TH
  • Thermocouples and Temperature Sensors Types J, K, N, T, E, R, S, B and C, plus PT100, PT500 and PT1000 RTDs, made to length. Part code TS
  • Ceramic Infrared Heaters Radiant surface heating, thermoforming, drying and preheating. Part code IR

Which machine are you heating?

Each industry page carries a process diagram with the heated zones marked and a table that maps the machine, zone by zone, to the element type that suits it.

  • Injection MouldingEvery zone on the machine, from the dryer to the mould, with the element type that suits it.
  • ExtrusionBarrel, adapter and die head heating for pipe, profile, sheet and film lines.
  • Blow MouldingPreform reheat, extrusion head and neck tooling heating for PET and extrusion blow lines.
  • Packaging MachinerySealing bars, cutting and creasing tools, shrink tunnels and hot melt tanks.
  • Die and MouldHot stamping dies, press platens and mould preheat, where the heater lives inside the tool.
  • Food ProcessingTanks, sealing tools, dryers and jacketed lines, in plant that gets washed down.
  • Pharmaceutical MachineryBlister and tube sealing, granulation drying, jacketed vessels and validated temperature measurement.
  • Rubber and ElastomersCuring and vulcanising platens, extruder barrels and mould heating.
  • Other Industrial HeatingOvens, tanks, ducts, pipes and surfaces, in plant that does not fit any of the categories above.

An enquiry should arrive as a specification

A general contact form asks for a name, a phone number and a message. Ours asks for a heater: diameter, length, heated length, wattage, voltage, termination, lead protection, thermocouple, and the bore tolerance in your own part.

That is the difference between a quotation that takes three phone calls and one that goes out the same day.
