# Provenance of the archived images

Captured 25 Aug 2026, completed from the live WordPress media library on
26 Aug 2026 (`/wp-json/wp/v2/media`, 35 originals). 61 files, which is every
original plus the WordPress generated thumbnails.

## These images cannot be used on the new site

Two separate problems, and either one on its own would be disqualifying.

**1. They are not Swiftheat's photographs.** `Cartridge-Heaters-2.png` carries a
visible **"DETAI"** watermark. DETAI is a Chinese heater manufacturer. The rest
of the set is the same generic trade catalogue photography that circulates
across supplier listings: identical white sweep, identical red silicone lead
boots, identical lighting. Publishing another manufacturer's product photography
as Swiftheat's own is a copyright exposure and a credibility risk, and it is
worse now than it was in 2024 because reverse image search makes it trivial to
spot.

**2. They are too small regardless.** The largest original is 835 x 835. Most sit
between 200 and 350 px. Nothing here is usable for a hero, a product page or a
catalogue at 2026 screen densities.

## What they are still good for

Reference, and only reference. They show what Swiftheat sells and what
accessories they offer, which is why the option catalogue on the new site names
right angle exits, flanges, mountable threads, silicone coated fibreglass
sleeve, braided metal sleeve and armour: all of those came off the old Products
page.

Every place a photograph belongs on the new site carries a dashed placeholder
with the shot written into it. That is the photography brief.
