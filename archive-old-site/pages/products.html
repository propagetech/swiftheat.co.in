Cartridge Heaters

01.

Versatile Applications

Cartridge heaters find applications across diverse industries like plastics, packaging, automotive, aerospace, food processing, and electronics manufacturing. Their compact design allows localized and precise heating for processes like injection molding, sealing, drying, and heating liquids.

02.

Customizable Solutions

We offer customized cartridge heaters tailored to your specific requirements. Customize the wattage, voltage, diameter, length, sheath material, lead protection, and accessories like right-angle exits and flanges to optimize performance.
03.

Efficient Heat Transfer

The cylindrical design ensures efficient heat transfer to the surrounding surfaces, enabling rapid heating and precise temperature control. This results in improved productivity, energy savings, and extended equipment life.

Cartridge Heater Accessories

Different accessories can be provided alongside the primary heater according to the specific demands of the application. These may include items like right-angle exits, right-angle blocks, T-strain clamps, round flanges, oval flanges, and more

Coil Heaters: Uniform and Rapid Heating

01.

Spiral Design for Efficiency

Coil heaters feature a spiral coiled heating element made of high-resistance wire. This compact design maximizes surface area contact, enabling uniform and rapid heating over a small area.
02.

Diverse Industrial Applications

Ideal for processes like plastic injection molding, extrusion, and hot runner systems, coil heaters ensure even heat distribution, reducing hot spots and improving product quality.
03.

Fast Heat-up and Responsiveness

The tight coil configuration allows for quick heat transfer, enabling fast heat-up times and responsive temperature control, optimizing process efficiency.

Band Heaters: Efficient Cylindrical Heating

Band heaters are a type of heating element used in various industrial applications to heat cylindrical surfaces such as pipes, barrels, and containers. Their versatility, durability, and effectiveness make them indispensable in various industries where precise and consistent heating is essential for operational success.

Types of Band Heaters:

01.

Mica Band Heaters

Feature a mica insulation layer, providing excellent thermal conductivity and insulation. Can be supplied With / Without TC hole
02.

Ceramic Band Heaters

Utilize ceramic insulation for high-temperature applications and efficient heat transfer. Can be supplied With / Without TC hole

Other features of Band Heaters

Efficiency

Band heaters provide efficient and uniform heating, ensuring optimal process performance.

Versatility

Available in various sizes, shapes, and configurations to suit different applications.

Durability

Constructed with robust materials, they offer long-term reliability even in harsh industrial environments.

Ease of Installation

Designed for easy installation and maintenance, minimizing downtime

Strip heaters

Strip heaters are designed to provide uniform heating over flat or curved surfaces and are available in various sizes, wattages, and configurations to suit different heating requirements.

Types of Strip Heaters:

01.

Fin Strip Heaters

Feature fins on one side to increase surface area and improve heat transfer efficiency.
02.

Ceramic Strip Heaters

Utilize ceramic insulation for high-temperature applications and efficient heat transfer.
03.

Clamp-on Strip Heaters

Designed to be clamped onto surfaces without the need for drilling or mounting holes.

Tubular heaters

Tubular heaters are versatile electric heating elements commonly used in industrial, commercial, and residential applications for heating air, gases, liquids, and solid surfaces

Types of Tubular Heaters:

01.

Immersion Heaters

Submerged directly into liquids for heating applications such as water heating, oil heating, and chemical processing.
02.

Air Heaters

Used for heating air in HVAC systems, ovens, drying cabinets, and other applications requiring forced air heating.
03.

Process Heaters

Designed for heating gases and liquids in industrial processes, including food processing, plastics manufacturing, and pharmaceutical production.
